RF-EPiano adds a RackForge adapter and a prepared silent-state reset to
the vendored DSP. It exposes the existing reed swing multiplier for the 200A
Bark program. The upstream synthesis and circuit calculations are unchanged.
The bundled engine uses melange-primitives (upstream pin 7ecb36c7).
https://github.com/hal0zer0/melange
